## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are company employees using the console to quickly get information from company knowledge. Their workflow is task-focused, repeat usage, and often time-constrained.

### Brand Personality
Not explicitly defined yet. Until clarified, prioritize functional clarity and restraint over expressive branding.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal and dark-mode-first. Preserve the existing Industrial Navy visual foundation and avoid decorative complexity. Prefer compact, high-signal layouts, consistent spacing, and low-noise surfaces that support fast scanning and fast decision-making.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize for speed: every screen should reduce time-to-information with clear hierarchy, concise copy, and predictable interaction patterns.
2. Keep it minimal: remove non-essential visual elements, prioritize whitespace discipline, and use motion only when it improves comprehension.
3. Dark-first consistency: treat dark mode as the default experience and maintain coherent contrast, elevation, and states across all components.
4. Accessibility by default: meet WCAG AA contrast and interaction requirements, including keyboard usability and visible focus states.
5. Information over ornament: design choices should increase readability, scannability, and confidence in data-heavy workflows.
